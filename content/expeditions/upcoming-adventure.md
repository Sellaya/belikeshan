The road does not stop after one loop. The next belikeshan expedition is **in process** — and this page will carry the follow-up as planning moves forward.

## Where things stand

Planning is active across four areas:

- **Route research** — potential corridors, distances, border and visa requirements, and the best seasonal window to ride
- **Gear & logistics** — refining the Suzuki DR650 setup, camping kit and solo overland loadout from lessons learned on past journeys
- **Documentary prep** — episode structure, camera workflow and the story belikeshan will tell from the road
- **Community updates** — this page and [@belikeshan](https://www.instagram.com/belikeshan/) will share milestones as the route becomes public

The destination and full route map will be announced once research is complete. Until then, check back here for planning updates.
