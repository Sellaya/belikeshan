There is a silence in the Himalayas that no camera can capture and no words can hold. You must ride into it to understand.

Winter on the Karakoram Highway is not for the unprepared. It is for those who have made peace with the edge — where asphalt meets ice, and the only companion is the sound of your own breath.

This journey taught me that solitude is not loneliness. It is clarity.
