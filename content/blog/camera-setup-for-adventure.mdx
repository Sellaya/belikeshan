Filming while riding solo is the hardest thing I do. There is no crew. No second takes. No one to hold the reflector.

Here is exactly how I capture cinematic footage on the road.

## The Core Kit

- **Sony FX3** — Primary camera. Compact, full-frame, incredible low light.
- **DJI RS 3 Pro** — Gimbal for walking shots and b-roll.
- **DJI Mavic 3 Pro** — Aerial perspectives that ground cameras cannot reach.
- **GoPro Hero 12** — Helmet cam and crash protection footage.

## Settings I Never Change

- 4K at 24fps for cinematic feel
- S-Log3 for maximum dynamic range
- Shutter at 1/50 for natural motion blur
- Auto ISO capped at 12,800

## The Workflow

Every evening at camp, I offload footage to two SSD drives. One stays with the bike. One stays on my person. Redundancy is not optional when you're 500km from the nearest town.

The best footage always comes when you stop trying to film and start living the moment. Then the camera becomes an extension of your eye.
