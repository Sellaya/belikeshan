Everyone asks about the motorcycle. Nobody asks about the mind.

Solo expedition riding is 20% physical and 80% psychological. Here is what I've learned after 40,000 kilometers alone.

## Solitude Is a Skill

You must learn to be alone without being lonely. The road will test this every single day.

## Decision Fatigue

When you're alone, every decision is yours. Where to camp. When to push. When to stop. By week three, this becomes exhausting. Build routines. Automate the small decisions.

## The 3-Day Rule

Homesickness peaks at day three. Always. If you can survive day three, you can survive the expedition.

## Connection

Paradoxically, solo riders often connect more deeply with people they meet. Without a group buffer, you are open to every interaction. Embrace this.

The mind is the most important piece of gear you'll carry. Train it like you train for altitude.
