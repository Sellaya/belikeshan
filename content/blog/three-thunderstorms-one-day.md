This is the raw side of solo expedition riding. No script. No second takes. Just the road, the sky turning black, and the decision to keep moving.

The full episode is on YouTube: **I Survived 3 Thunderstorms in 1 Day | USA Loop Day 7**.

These are the moments that do not make it into statistics — 25 states, 10,000 miles, 33 days — but they are the moments that make the documentary real.
